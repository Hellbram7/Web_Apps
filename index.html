Document & Photo Utilities

Simple tools for school and form-filling work. Pick one below. Everything runs in your browser and your files never leave your device.

01Print

ID Card Print Tool

Print both sides of an Aadhaar or PAN card on one A4 page.

You give
Photos of the card front and back
You get
A straightened, sharpened A4 sheet at exact card size, with cut marks

02Extract

Photo & Signature Extractor

Pull your photo and signature out of a scanned form or document.

You give
One image of the document
You get
A clean photo and signature as separate files, compressed to a size limit for online forms
Open tool→
03Capture

Passport Photo Maker

Take a passport photo with your camera and change its background colour.

You give
A live camera shot or any photo of a person
You get
A cropped 35 × 45 mm photo on white, blue or any colour, saved at your chosen KB size, plus a 4×6 print sheet
